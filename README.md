# Prinita Kandasamy — proof site

A pitch site to show Prinita, built in the spare register of an actor’s personal site (see [zendaya.com](https://www.zendaya.com/)), with the three rooms she needs: acting, media presenting, and dance.

It is not her official site. Most photographs are stand-ins. The isiThunzi poster is a public film image. A few of her Instagram posts are linked, and load in the browser only if Instagram allows it. Stats and presenting credits stay blank on purpose.

Spelling follows her public name, Prinita Kandasamy (`@prinita_kandasamy`), not the folder name.

## What is confirmed

- Cast, [isiThunzi](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt39046281/) (2024, horror short, AFDA, filmed in Johannesburg). Character not named on the public credit.
- Cast, [Echoes of the Bell](https://films.afda.co.za/film/1374/Echoes-of-the-bell/) (2024, mystery short, AFDA). Character not named on the public credit.
- Instagram and [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm17920918/).

Nothing else was filled in to make the pages look finished.

## How to show it

Open the home page. The gold-free bar at the top tells her the photos are not hers. Hide it if you want a cleaner first look; the stand-in captions stay.

Walk: home, reels, acting, presenting, dance, about, press kit, contact.

## When she approves

- Replace files in `assets/` with her photographs. Keep the same names or update the `src` in the HTML.
- Put reel URLs in the empty players on `reels.html`, `acting.html`, `presenting.html`, and `dance.html`.
- Rename the dance tabs to her real styles.
- Replace the about copy and the spec dashes with what she confirms.
- Add the agency name in every footer.
- Point the contact form at her booking address, and remove `noindex` plus `robots.txt` when it should be found.
