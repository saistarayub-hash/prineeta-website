About

She asked to tell this herself. What is here is only what is already public, plus the blanks a casting director expects to find.

Prinita Kandasamy is an actor, a media presenter, and a dancer. On Instagram she describes herself as a dancer and an AFDA graduate. Her own line there: stay humble, live simple. Her public posts include Bharatanatyam, and a Red Bull Dance Your Style qualifier in Johannesburg.

On screen she is billed first on the 2024 horror short isiThunzi, filmed in Johannesburg. A public post on that film credits her as Chitra. She is also in the cast of Echoes of the Bell. Height, eye colour, and the rest of the life are still not filled in. That paragraph is hers to replace.
